# Sources

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License, bundled through Fontsource)

- EB Garamond: body text
- Noto Serif Display: headings
- Ponomar: Church Slavonic display lines (Russian)
- GFS Didot: Greek display lines

## Images

All artwork is drawn in SVG and CSS for this template: book covers, icon placeholders, prayer ropes, crosses, goods, map and logo. No photographs or third-party images are used. Icon placeholders are marked "ICON PHOTO PLACEHOLDER" and should be replaced with photographs of the icons the store sells.

## Texts

Titles of public-domain patristic works (for example The Ladder of Divine Ascent, The Philokalia, The Way of a Pilgrim) appear as sample listings only. Editions, translators, publishers, prices and ISBNs are placeholders. All other copy is short placeholder text written for this template.
